The face66 facial targets
MPFB · floating head
CC0 · public domain
A mint-green floating head with a separate Otohiko wig, luminous eyes, and preserved facial shape keys.

For best results, choose a GLB that has facial morph targets (blendshapes) and faces forward. Unsupported models will still render, but may not lip-sync.

About speech: OpenAI's streamed PCM is played through the laptop's PulseAudio output; the iPad receives compact jaw-envelope values and Rhubarb mouth cues to animate the face, never the speech audio. The same PCM is analyzed for lip-sync on the laptop. Visitor microphone audio is not saved; recognized visitor transcripts are retained privately in logs/visitor_transcripts.jsonl. Generated speech audio and temporary Rhubarb files are discarded after analysis; the API key stays on this local server and is never sent to the browser.
